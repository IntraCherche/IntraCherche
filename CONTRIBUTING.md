# Contributing to IntraCherche

Thank you for considering a contribution. IntraCherche is licensed under the EUPL-1.2 (see `LICENSE`).

## Before a larger change

Open an issue first and describe what you want to change. This saves you work on something that
does not fit the project's direction (an on-premise search engine for sensitive documents: no data
leaves the organisation, no cloud dependency).

## Rights on your contribution

By submitting a contribution (pull request, patch), you confirm that:

1. you wrote it, or you have the right to submit it under the EUPL-1.2;
2. it is licensed under the EUPL-1.2 like the rest of the project;
3. you grant the project maintainer the right to also distribute it under the licences the EUPL-1.2
   lists as compatible, and to relicense it should a later version of the EUPL replace this one.

Sign off each commit (`git commit -s`) to state this.

## Practicalities

- Build and test as described in `README.md` (section Building) and the header of `distribution/build-distrib.sh`.
- Keep a change focused, with a test when behaviour changes, and add one short line to `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Security problems: do not open a public issue; write to contact@intracherche.fr.
